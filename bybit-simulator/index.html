Rules and limitations

  • Spot BTCUSDT, long-only, no leverage or shorting.
  • 1H EMA50 above EMA200 trend filter. A also requires a 15m EMA20 reclaim, RSI14 crossing above 50 and volume above 20-bar average.
  • B adds a completed 4H close above 4H EMA200. C requires a pullback into the EMA20–EMA50 zone and bullish recovery. D adds volume at least 1.2× its 20-bar average.
  • Stops use the latest confirmed 3-left/3-right pivot low and at least 0.75 ATR14 distance. Entry is next 15m candle open. Target is 2R.
  • Same-candle stop/target collision resolves stop-first. Fees and slippage are charged on both sides.
  • Exchange minimum notional is $5; trades are skipped if meeting it would breach the risk budget.
  • Backtest is a research prototype, not evidence of a profitable edge. Data depth is limited by the selected fetch window; this is not a robust out-of-sample validation.
